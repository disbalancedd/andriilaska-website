Two years of training in print mean I know what it takes for menus, posters and flyers to look just as good on paper. For me, design shouldn’t just look good — it should bring you customers.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a website cost?+

A one-page website starts at €590, a website with up to 5 pages at €990. After our first call you get a fixed price — and it stays fixed even if I need longer.

How quickly will it be ready?+

A menu or poster usually takes a few days, a website about 1–2 weeks — once texts and photos are ready.

Do I need a domain and hosting?+

Yes, and I’m happy to take care of it: hosting, updates and small changes for €29 a month. The domain is registered in your name, so it always belongs to you.

What do you need from me?+

Your logo (if you have one), the key information and prices. I can write the texts for you and take the photos on site.

Can I have things changed later?+

Of course — new prices, opening hours or a new seasonal menu are updated quickly and easily.
